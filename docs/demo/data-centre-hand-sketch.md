# Data Centre whiteboard demo

Sign in and open **Whiteboard → Sample whiteboards → Data Centre — Hand Sketch → Use sample**. EasyDraw creates a private editable copy and prefills **Describe your idea** with the prompt below. Choose **Generate preview**, review the result in 2D and 3D, and choose **Create diagram** only after the preview is acceptable. The built-in sample remains available to every account.

The standalone [PNG sketch](data-centre-hand-sketch.png) can also be opened with **File → Open image…** on an existing Whiteboard; for this manual path, paste the prompt yourself.

```text
Turn this top-view hand sketch into a clean, editable data-centre floor-plan diagram in English. Keep the three zones and their positions: Compute Hall on the left, Infrastructure on the right, and Network Operations Centre across the bottom. In Compute Hall, show three CRAC cooling units, two separate rows of five server racks labelled A-01 to A-05 and B-01 to B-05, and a cold aisle between the rows marked 18–22°C. In Infrastructure, show UPS A, UPS B, PDU, Spine A, Spine B, and SAN 01 to SAN 03. Connect Spine A to each Row A rack with teal Fabric A cables. Connect Spine B to each Row B rack and all three SAN units with blue Fabric B cables. In the operations centre, show Access, Monitoring, Network and Security. Use separate editable shapes and text for every device and real connector edges for the cables. Preserve the floor-plan layout and labels; make it neat and easy to present. Do not add devices, direction arrows on the physical cables, or a generic software flowchart.
```

The sketch is a simplified teaching/demo input derived from the structure of the dashboard Data Centre sample. AI recognition is approximate. The 3D preview can give editable shapes depth, but the prompt does not install the sample's bespoke 3D equipment recipes. Use the dashboard sample itself when an exact match to its 3D racks and equipment is required.
